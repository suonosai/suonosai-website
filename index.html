into power

Suono Sai is an Indian-Nigerian artist from Lagos who moves between Afro-Pop and Hip-Hop at full speed. His records carry two cultural inheritances in one voice, and he keeps releasing: singles and projects stacked into a growing catalogue.

Para Boy is the stance behind it all. Pressure is carried in public, styled instead of hidden, and turned into records. His site says the fan base grew fast enough to put three shows into a single night.

Afro-Pop structure. Hip-Hop instinct.
